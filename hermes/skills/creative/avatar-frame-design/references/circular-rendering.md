# Precise circular frame rendering

Use this recipe when the inner profile aperture and outside canvas must both be true alpha-zero, while the annular design progresses through several tiers.

1. Draw at 2x or higher resolution with Pillow and NumPy. With center `(cx,cy)`, compute `r = hypot(x-cx,y-cy)` and a common outer radius. Give early tiers a larger inner radius and later tiers a smaller one; keep enough canvas padding beyond the outer radius. Establish the palette before material shading and use the same three brand hues on every asset. Supporting grayscale/silver highlights do not create extra tier colors.
2. Render a softly graded annular base and draw arcs, inlaid sectors, radial seams and light sensors on a separate RGBA overlay. Design different *topologies* (single rail, dual rail, split arc, plate relay, orbit, phase, interwoven track, precision sectors, calibrated ticks, crown), not merely different bevels. Increase ring width and high-contrast plate pattern visibly across levels; subpixel sensor dots disappear when used as avatars.
3. After all overlays and glows, force alpha to zero wherever `r` lies outside the annulus or inside the profile aperture, and clear RGB at zero alpha. Downsample with Lanczos to the requested PNG size. Clipping only the base layer is insufficient: a late overlay can silently fill the hole or break the circular outer contour.
4. Verify every exported file by decoding it: PNG/RGBA, dimensions, `alpha[center] == 0`, zero alpha at all four canvas edges, zero alpha at every point safely inside the inner radius and beyond the outer radius, nonzero annular pixels, and unique hashes across the requested count. Lanczos may leave antialias values about 3-4 pixels across the mathematical boundary at final size; allow that narrow tolerance rather than demanding a binary edge. Compare each frame's bounding box and opaque annular area to the tier plan.
5. Preview the complete set on both dark and light mattes and at small profile size. If adjacent frames look like one repeated gold ring, revise the major annulus widths and segment architecture; more tiny ticks will not fix indistinguishable silhouettes. Confirm the accent remains restrained and no dark backing has flattened the transparent aperture. Deliver each PNG separately, never the contact sheet unless requested.
