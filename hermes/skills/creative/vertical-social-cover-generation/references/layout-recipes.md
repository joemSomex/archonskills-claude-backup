# Layout recipes

Use these as optional starting points. Adapt them to the supplied design references and keep every effect non-semantic.

1. **Asymmetric particle portrait** — three-quarter face on one side; large opposite negative space; particle or point-cloud edge dissolution; restrained accent light.
2. **Low-angle monumental close-up** — chin and neck create a strong upward line; face in the upper field; vertical abstract light strands; deep shadow below.
3. **Centered split-color close-up** — frontal eye contact; asymmetric cool/warm side light; soft bokeh; smoke or dust confined to one corner.
4. **High-contrast diagonal occlusion** — face in the upper half; narrow light slice across the eyes; non-semantic diagonal foreground plane; clean dark lower field.
5. **Neon profile with foreground depth** — profile or three-quarter face in an upper corner; saturated rim light; heavily defocused glass facets and circular bokeh in the opposite foreground.
6. **Profile dissolution** — clean side profile on one side; face-facing negative space; hair and shoulder dissolve into mist or particles; one restrained background light arc.

## Diversity gate

Before generating, compare every pair. Change at least three of: face angle, subject side, crop, scale, negative-space side, key-light direction, palette, foreground, background depth, or effect family. Reject plans that differ only by color grade, small translation, or crop.

## Text-leak gate

Poster references with large typography often cause text leakage. Repeat the ban in every generation prompt and inspect clothing, particles, borders, foreground blur, and lower negative space for pseudo-letters—not only obvious title zones.
