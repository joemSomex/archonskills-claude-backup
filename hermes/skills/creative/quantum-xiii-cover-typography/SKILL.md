## Required numeral asset gate

Before replacing an episode number, first run the numeral provenance recovery above. If the style was not agent-generated or its generation provenance cannot be recovered, require at least one of these:

1. The original editable title source containing the requested numeral: PSD, AI, SVG, layered design file, or editable text layer.
2. The exact font used for the episode number, plus enough source information to reproduce weight, slant, tracking, outline, bevel, extrusion, texture, cracks, shadow, and glow.
3. An approved cover or transparent glyph asset that already contains the requested numeral in the same style.
4. A verified complete numeral sheet `0-9` from the original designer.

If neither recovered generation provenance nor any listed asset exists, exact numeral replacement is not possible from the flattened `06` raster alone. Stop and tell the user what is missing. Offer an explicitly labeled approximation only after the user accepts that limitation. Never draw an unseen digit from hand-built polygons, splice it from another numeral, or repeatedly resize an invented glyph and present it as the approved typeface. A zero-difference result outside the edit region proves pixel preservation only; it does not prove font, glyph, texture, or proportion fidelity.

## Method

1. Load `creative:pixel-preserving-image-compositing` and follow its preservation checks.
2. For the established 1086x1448 cover composition, use an approved Episode 06 or 07 cover as the full base and change only the exact episode-number rectangle `(720, 905, 1005, 1105)`. Never reconstruct, re-mask, recolor, re-extract, or re-composite `量子纪元` or `QUANTUM XIII`; those pixels must remain byte-identical to the approved reference. Generate the complete requested number pair as one unit from the approved numeral samples and composite its complete localized crop into that rectangle with only a narrow feather at the outer boundary.
3. Preserve the complete approved effect stack, not only red face pixels. A strict red-dominance matte can discard the dark bevel, black keyline, down-right extrusion, cast shadow, dim red bloom, low-luminance lightning, sparks, embers, and smoky background interaction. When the original or regenerated numeral crop already contains the correct surrounding effects, composite the complete localized crop back into the exact source rectangle with a narrow feathered outer boundary, then verify that no rectangular seam appears. Use a glyph-only matte only when all discarded dark and low-luminance effects are separately rebuilt and visually matched.
4. Pass the required numeral asset gate before changing an episode number. Use the requested glyph from the original font, editable source, approved cover, verified numeral sheet, or recovered original image-generation workflow. Do not infer a `7` from the shapes of `0` and `6`.
5. Apply the approved numeral material to the authentic glyph without changing its geometry. Then compare cap height, width, stroke thickness, slant, baseline, spacing, bevel, extrusion, texture, cracks, shadow, and glow at native size against the adjacent approved digit. Use measurements as rejection gates, not as a substitute for authentic glyph geometry.
6. Keep every non-title pixel unchanged outside the localized title zone and verify `outside_changed_pixels=0`.
7. Visually confirm exact wording, complete strokes, no old title, no clipping, no rectangular patch, no source-scene contamination, no invented or mismatched numeral geometry, and no missing effect layer. Compare the new number against the approved reference for face texture, black fissures, chipped edges, upper-left highlight, dark lower-right bevel, outer keyline, extrusion, cast shadow, glow, lightning veins, particles, embers, and smoky background integration.
