# Branded presentation decks

## When to Use

Use for a company brand guide, story-world identity deck, faction/location visual system, or an update that adds approved brand artwork to an editable presentation. Pair with `powerpoint` for file operations and the relevant canon skill for factual claims.

## Procedure

1. **Discover before designing.** Inspect existing brand files, approved logos, artwork, previous decks, design tokens, and applicable story/product sources. Establish audience, format, and what is an existing approved identity versus a new proposal. For story-world decks, read the governing canon index and corrected source documents; distinguish source facts from proposed art direction on the slides themselves.
2. **Set a visual hierarchy and recognition system.** Keep parent-company identity separate from a franchise, faction, or location system. Choose a compact palette and type hierarchy; use one idea per slide and vary composition intentionally. For each character group and place, translate the user's signature cues into production-useful silhouette, materials, construction, surface texture, light behavior, scale, architecture, and treatment-to-avoid rules; a palette alone does not establish recognizability. Check exceptions and individual variants against the governing sources rather than making every member share an identical material, color, or glow. Label invented taglines, hex values, and visual interpretations as proposals until approved. A user's approval of a direction does not supply missing masters, usage rights, or public-facing copy approvals.
3. **Lock the delivery surface before building.** If the user says "canvas slideshow" and the target could be either Canva or a browser canvas, ask once; do not substitute an HTML canvas for an editable Canva presentation. For local editable decks, prefer PPTX with native text/shapes and separate images. For Canva, import that PPTX into the user's workspace when authenticated access exists. A Canva-compatible PPTX is not a Canva design: only provide a Canva share link after the import and an exact-design readback verify it. Preserve earlier deck versions on substantial revisions; keep slide numbers and source/status cues subordinate. Do not fabricate a logo or wordmark to fill an empty asset slot.
4. **Integrate supplied assets accurately.** Inspect each image before placing it. Keep the original binary as a durable copy with matching checksum when it will be reused; embed it without repainting or changing its aspect ratio. If a JPEG logo has a black field, place that field intentionally instead of pretending it is transparent; leave typography outside the mark. Request a transparent or vector master only for uses that need it. A supplied logo is not evidence that every proposed palette or line of brand copy is official.
5. **Export and visually verify.** Read back the final PPTX slide count, expected sections, embedded-image count, and text after the last revision; compare the PDF page count when exporting a preview. Render with an actual available renderer; if a bespoke PDF renderer is used, verify its text sizing and images independently because PPTX paragraph styling and run styling may differ, and a successful export does not prove visual parity. Inspect every slide in a contact sheet and inspect the cover, dense layouts, image-heavy pages, newly added evidence slides, and final source/approval slide at presentation scale. For produced-media stills, retain an exact episode/time provenance label and explain what the frame proves versus what the proposed direction extrapolates; a single skyline angle is not an atlas. Correct clipping, overlap, stretched imagery, or unreadable typography before delivery.
6. **Verify the actual target.** For Canva delivery, open the authenticated workspace, import the PPTX, inspect the converted slide count, text editability, image placement and font substitutions, and read back the resulting design URL before claiming completion. If authentication is required, use the approved vault or interactive login flow; never imply that a local PPTX was uploaded. If access is unavailable, provide the importable source and clearly label the live Canva design as incomplete rather than repeating an upload instruction as though it finished the task.
7. **Deliver with precise status.** Send the editable deck and preview/export when available. State which assets were embedded and which remain missing; keep lore accuracy, approved brand identity, and newly proposed colors distinct. Do not claim a logo was added until the exported slides visibly contain it.

## User-facing conventions

- Use exact names and ASCII-safe punctuation for copy that will be reused in chat or deck source.
- Keep the report brief: artifact links, verified scope, and only the outstanding decisions or assets.
