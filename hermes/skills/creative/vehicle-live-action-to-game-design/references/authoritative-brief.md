# Authoritative vehicle conversion brief

Create 10 separate, unique PNG images that convert the vehicle in @image1 into a premium Arcane-inspired animated vehicle design sheet, while preserving its exact identity, silhouette, proportions, construction, materials, and key details.
Use @image1 as the source of truth. The vehicle must remain immediately recognizable. Do not redesign its function, alter its proportions, remove important features, or add unrelated design elements.
The result must look like the same vehicle translated into the visual language of Arcane: cinematic, painterly, sculptural, and clearly three-dimensional. Use simplified but believable forms, hand-painted textures, graphic light-and-shadow shapes, controlled edge highlights, rich colored shadows, subtle surface wear, and a refined animated-series finish. The final result must not look photorealistic, anime, Disney, Pixar, or generic CGI.
Each variation must be generated directly from @image1, not from a previous generation. Produce 10 individual files, not a collage.
DESIGN SHEET LAYOUT
Each PNG must be presented as a clean vehicle design sheet on a light neutral studio background.
Include these views of the same vehicle in every image:
A main front or 3/4 hero view
A side profile view
A rear or back profile view
A close-up detail view
All views must show the exact same vehicle design and remain fully consistent in shape, structure, materials, and detailing.
VEHICLE-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS
Preserve the vehicle’s:
overall body shape and silhouette
wheel size and placement
proportions and stance
paneling and construction
windows, doors, lights, mirrors, grille, vents, trim, and mechanical details
surface materials and visible wear patterns where applicable
If the vehicle includes exposed mechanical parts, interior-visible areas, armor plating, or custom attachments, keep them consistent and clearly recognizable across all views.
MATERIAL AND RENDERING DIRECTION
Render each vehicle material with clear separation and stylized readability. Metal, glass, rubber, plastic, painted bodywork, leather, fabric, chrome, or other vehicle surfaces should each react differently to light while remaining painterly and cohesive.
Use dramatic but controlled cinematic lighting, subtle rim light, soft atmospheric depth, and strong sculptural shading to make the vehicle feel premium and production-ready.
VARIATION DIRECTION
Across the 10 variations, keep the vehicle’s identity completely locked while varying only:
the painterly treatment
lighting approach
texture handling
shading style
degree of stylization
Do not change the vehicle itself.
Do not create a collage, moodboard, or multiple unrelated vehicles.
