# QUANTUM XIII glossary

Use these established translations consistently in Chinese story synopses:

- `Quantum` → `机核战士`
- `Kioku` → `岐奥窟`
- `memory bank` / contextually shortened `bank` → `记忆库`
- Character names such as `Kara`, `Silas`, `Noel`, and `Sylvia` remain in English.

## Voice reference

Write concise cinematic synopsis prose with clear causal flow. A useful paragraph structure is:

1. The characters follow a clue to the location and define what the location is.
2. Reveal the owner’s relationship to a character and state the condition for access.
3. Establish why entry is dangerous, then land on the arrival of unexpected guests.

Use natural Chinese relationship language such as “是旧识” and suspense language such as “不速之客悄然抵达”. Do not mechanically preserve English clause order.
