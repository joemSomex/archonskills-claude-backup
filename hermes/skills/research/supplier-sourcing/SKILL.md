# Supplier Sourcing

Find credible source factories for a product, especially when the user supplies a marketplace or video link and constrains the city or region.

## Procedure

1. **Identify the product before searching suppliers.**
   - Open the supplied page in a real browser when the page is JavaScript-heavy.
   - Record the brand, model, marketplace identifier, product category, distinctive shape, materials, dimensions, power, connectivity version, and any uncommon feature.
   - Follow linked product pages or reputable reseller mirrors when a video page exposes only a title.
   - Treat product titles as search leads, not proof of the OEM.

2. **Search exact-match fingerprints in parallel.**
   - Search the model, marketplace identifier, exact specification phrases, distinctive appearance terms, and English/Chinese product names.
   - Search B2B platforms and indexed supplier pages with queries such as:
     - `"<model>" manufacturer supplier`
     - `"<distinctive feature>" factory China`
     - `site:made-in-china.com <product phrase>`
     - `site:alibaba.com/product-detail <product phrase>`
     - `site:1688.com <Chinese product phrase> <city>`
   - When a platform search requires login, pivot to search-engine indexing and public company/product pages rather than presenting an inaccessible result as verified.

3. **Verify the strongest candidate across independent pages.**
   - Require an exact or near-exact product listing plus a company profile that labels the business as `Manufacturer/Factory`, not merely a storefront.
   - Compare product geometry and specifications, not only generic category words.
   - Cross-check the factory address, establishment date, employee count, OEM/ODM claims, certifications, and contact channel on at least two public sources where possible.
   - Prefer the producer of the specialized subsystem when the finished product combines a commodity component with distinctive technology; this often identifies the real OEM better than searching generic factories.

4. **Respect the requested geography without forcing a false match.**
   - Divide findings into:
     1. verified exact/closest source factory;
     2. local same-category manufacturers;
     3. traders or unverified leads.
   - If the exact factory is outside the requested city, say so plainly. Do not relabel a nearby-city factory or local reseller as a local source factory.
   - A local company selling a related model is only a development or sourcing alternative until it proves current production capability.

5. **Check commercial and compliance readiness.**
   - Ask for a live factory/production video, current business license, recent shipment evidence, sample price, MOQ, tier pricing, lead time, customization fees, packaging options, warranty, and tooling ownership.
   - For electronics, request applicable CE/FCC/RoHS reports and battery UN38.3/MSDS documents.
   - Flag recognizable entertainment characters, logos, or trade dress as potential IP risk; request authorization or a non-infringing redesign.
   - Use `references/factory-verification.md` for the supplier validation checklist and outreach fields.

6. **Deliver a decision-oriented answer in chat.**
   - Lead with the conclusion: whether an exact factory was found in the requested location.
   - Rank no more than a few useful candidates. For each, give match level, company type, city/address, product link, contact route, and what still needs verification.
   - Separate confirmed facts from marketplace claims and stale directory data.
   - Include inline citations for external claims and a source list when the user needs to contact or audit suppliers.
   - Keep the response practical: recommended first contact, local backup, and next verification steps. Do not create a document unless requested.

## Pitfalls

- **Do not equate an Amazon brand or reseller name with the factory** — private-label listings often conceal the OEM.
- **Do not treat a generic audio factory as evidence it can build magnetic-levitation audio** — the levitation system is the differentiating production capability.
- **Do not call a directory entry current without qualification** — old B2B pages can preserve discontinued models and stale phone numbers.
- **Do not claim an exact match from title similarity alone** — compare images, dimensions, electronics, base geometry, and uncommon features.
- **Do not satisfy a city constraint by lowering the evidence standard** — report the nearest verified exact factory and label local alternatives honestly.
