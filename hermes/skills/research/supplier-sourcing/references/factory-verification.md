# Factory Verification Checklist

Use this after finding a plausible supplier. The goal is to distinguish an operating factory from a trader, stale listing, or catalog aggregator.

## Evidence to request

- Current business license with legal company name and factory address
- Timestamped video showing exterior signage, production line, assembly, testing, warehouse, and the target product
- Video call walkthrough from the listed address
- Recent redacted purchase order, bill of lading, or shipment record for the same product family
- Factory audit report (SGS, TÜV, BV, Intertek, or platform audit), including audit date and audited legal entity
- Product test reports issued to the same legal entity or an explained affiliate

## Commercial fields

Ask for these explicitly so quotations are comparable:

- Exact product/model and reference image
- EXW and FOB price at sample, 100, 500, and 1,000 units
- MOQ for stock product, logo customization, color change, packaging, and structural modification
- Sample charge and whether it is refundable after an order
- Sample lead time, production lead time, monthly capacity, and peak-season capacity
- Tooling fee, mold ownership, exclusivity, and design-file ownership
- Payment terms, warranty period, defect allowance, spare-unit policy, and after-sales process
- Carton dimensions, gross/net weight, units per carton, HS code, and FOB port

## Electronics and battery documents

Request only those applicable to the destination market and product:

- CE and RoHS for the EU
- FCC test report or authorization for the US radio product
- Bluetooth qualification information where relevant
- UN38.3, MSDS/SDS, and transport documents for lithium batteries
- Adapter safety approvals matching the destination market
- Test-report number, lab, issue date, model coverage, and applicant name

## Match grading

- **Exact verified factory:** exact product and current production evidence; legal entity and address cross-checked.
- **Probable OEM:** exact product listing and manufacturer profile, but production evidence still pending.
- **Local development alternative:** relevant technical capability in the requested location, but no exact-product proof.
- **Trader/unverified lead:** sells the product or category without credible production evidence.

Never collapse these grades into a single “factory” list.
