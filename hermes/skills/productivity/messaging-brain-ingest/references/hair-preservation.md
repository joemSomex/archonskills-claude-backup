# Hair Preservation and Alpha-Mask Rules

Use the following instructions word for word whenever applicable:

```text
HAIR PRESERVATION — HIGHEST PRIORITY
- Preserve the complete original hair silhouette exactly as shown in the source image.
- Preserve every visible hair section, including the main hair mass, windblown locks, flyaway strands, detached wisps, backlit hairs, soft edge hairs, and semi-transparent hair tips.
- The long windblown strands extending outward on the right side of the character are part of the character and must not be removed, shortened, simplified, reshaped, or mistaken for background haze.
- Hair overlapping glow, haze, particles, or colours similar to the background must still be retained.
- Preserve the original length, direction, curvature, spacing, colour variation, transparency, and softness of all hair strands.
- Do not reconstruct, repaint, invent, or replace any missing hair.

ALPHA-MASK RULES
- Use soft alpha matting around hair. Do not use a hard silhouette cutout.
- Do not shrink, contract, erode, simplify, or aggressively clean the hair mask.
- Do not classify fine hair, windblown strands, flyaway hairs, or semi-transparent hair tips as background.
- Preserve partially transparent edge pixels wherever they belong to the original hair.
- Remove background pixels only when they are clearly separate from the hair, character, or retained particle effect.
- If a pixel may contain both hair and background, preserve the hair contribution with partial alpha rather than deleting the entire pixel.
- Removing background haze must never remove or weaken hair strands that overlap that haze.
- “Clean edges” means removing colour contamination while preserving the complete original hair geometry. It does not mean trimming or simplifying the hair.
- Before export, compare the extracted hair silhouette against the source image and confirm that every visible windblown lock and flyaway strand remains present.
```
