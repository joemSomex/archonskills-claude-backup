# Quantum 13 source-to-calendar repurposing checklist

Adapted from the user-provided `content-repurposer.md`; this is a working procedure, not a generic platform quota. Use before committing a highlight cut to a calendar slot.

1. **Source and standalone value:** Record the episode, exact clip ID, opening anchor, and what the finished cut actually shows. Ask whether a first-time viewer can understand the moment without prior episode context. If not, propose a short context line, recut, or a different clip; do not invent missing dialogue or scene action. Only released-episode facts in audience-facing claims; a non-spoiler glimpse of the next episode needs approved material. When surveying a full episode for future derivatives, try identifying 3-7 standalone moments/ideas and 3-7 viable vertical-cut candidates as an ideation exercise; do not force a count, invent scenes, or imply six existing cuts need to be re-edited.
2. **Audience-specific adaptation:** Reuse a clean, unwatermarked master while choosing an appropriate opening treatment, cover/thumbnail, subtitle treatment, audience prompt, and route to the full episode for each platform. Treat 30-90 seconds as a trial range for potential new short-form cuts, not a platform rule or reason to trim an existing finished clip; compare actual Somex retention and completion before favoring a duration. Preserve Somex's cinematic identity; do not assume a deliberately low-polish TikTok style is appropriate. Keep exact user/source wording when quoted. Provide a platform-specific caption *brief* if useful, but leave caption copy, character counts, and hashtag optimization to the separate caption skill when it exists. This is a plan for variants, not permission to alter a finalized cut silently.
3. **Asset and effort gate:** Label what is already ready versus needed: clip master, vertical crop, subtitle pass, cover/still, caption brief for handoff, rights check, approval, and moderation. A still/image post or Story based on a clip requires an actual usable frame or approved graphic; do not pretend it already exists. For a draft schedule, mark missing items rather than blocking the proposal.
4. **Freshness and chronology ledger:** Track clip ID, episode number, angle, platform, last-posted date, and status. Shuffle clip numbers within each episode so overlapping followers do not receive identical repeats, while allowing a cut to introduce new viewers on another platform. Preserve episode order in the overall calendar: no Episode 06 highlight before any remaining planned Episode 05 highlight, on any platform. Avoid duplicate uploads to the same platform without a meaningful new angle. Do not claim all platform audiences are disjoint.
5. **Placement and measurement:** Decide the derivative's purpose (new-viewer discovery, conversation, return to episode) before placing it. Apply the fixed Friday 23:00 GMT+8 Community slot, per-platform nonconsecutive **video** rule, and Instagram/TikTok different-date **video** rule. Stories/polls are exempt from these video restrictions. Draft angles and production briefs for review; do not draft optimized captions/hashtags as a calendar deliverable. Review each platform's actual performance relative to its own prior posts and episode-viewing objective.

The source file's 3-7 ideas/clips and 30-90-second range are approved as **experiments**, never hard quotas. Do not adopt its character/hashtag prescriptions, mandatory `#shorts`, global 24-hour spacing, generic platform leverage hierarchy, or BlackTwist references as Somex policy. Caption and hashtag optimization belongs to a separate future skill; do not invent or require that skill before it exists. Facebook is now live and in scope; LinkedIn/X/Pinterest are outside the approved calendar.
