# Somex Studios - Quantum 13 Content Calendar

Build a usable, source-backed publishing schedule for Quantum 13 only. Use "Quantum 13" in new audience-facing calendar copy; preserve original source filenames and exact quoted titles without silently renaming them. This skill is approved for use and remains editable as the user provides new references or changes channel rules. Treat each new calendar as a proposal until separately approved or scheduled.

## When to Use

Trigger on "Somex content calendar", "schedule our posts", "plan next week's content", and "content calendar for Quantum 13". Use for Somex publishing plans, not a generic social-media strategy or an instruction to post immediately.

## Before planning

- Read `references/current-channel-context.md` for the user's concrete cadence, channel status, and current clip inventory. Read `references/analytics-and-scheduling.md` for the user's weekday data interpretation, supplied export paths, and mandatory calendar checks. Reconfirm time-sensitive asset availability before making a new plan.
- Read `references/social-source-notes.md` and `references/content-calendar-source-notes.md` for ideas supplied in `schedule-skill.md` and `content-calendar.md`; treat generic frequency, percentages, and best-time claims as suggestions to verify, not Somex commitments.
- Read user-provided brand, episode, campaign, audience, scheduling, and analytics files available for this request. Check for `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `.claude/product-marketing.md`, or `product-marketing-context.md` if a project directory is supplied; do not imply any of these files exists without checking.
- Reuse `waterfall-content-strategy` for episode-to-asset extraction and `youtube-long-form-strategy` for detailed YouTube advice. Do not copy their full contents here.
- Ask only for facts that would change the content recommendations: campaign/episode dates, audience objective, existing assets, spoiler policy, and approval owner if actual scheduling is requested. Do not ask for weekly production capacity or tailor the number of ideas to an assumed workload; the user will adjust proposed content to fit their team. Group any other essential questions. Weekly and monthly are both supported; the user chooses per request. If dates are unknown, label provisional assumptions rather than inventing a release timetable.

## Build the calendar

1. Plan by week or month according to the request; if unspecified, ask or offer a clearly labeled provisional horizon. Set dates and daily boundaries in GMT+8. Identify source assets, release dates, spoilers/embargoes, and any immovable events. Distinguish confirmed dates from suggestions.
2. Extract content atoms from approved source material: scenes, characters, quotations, audience questions, music, production insights, and visuals. Start with the six user-listed Episode 05/06 highlight cuts. Apply `references/repurposing-checklist.md`: test standalone value, try 3-7 candidate moments/cuts when surveying a full episode, trial 30-90 seconds for *new* short-form cuts, adapt opening angle/cover per platform, mark assets needed versus ready, and track clip/angle freshness by platform. **Shuffle cut numbers within an episode, but keep the overall episode rollout chronological across all platforms: finish the planned Episode 05 highlights before the first Episode 06 highlight.** Treat counts and length as experiments, never quotas or reasons to recut a finished asset. Leave caption and hashtag optimization to a separate future skill; supply only a caption brief when needed. Opening quotes and durations alone do not establish the full cut. Do not fabricate a moment, timestamp, audience result, or canon claim. Use released episodes for audience-facing facts; allow only user-approved non-spoiler glimpses of the next episode and separate those from viewer theories or unpublished drafts.
3. Assign each idea an objective (discovery, community conversation, episode viewing, returning viewers), content pillar, specific angle, native format, destination, source asset, spoiler level, proposed date/time, required production asset if any, and measurable signal. Recommend useful content without limiting ideas to an assumed weekly workload. Distinguish ready assets from new work so the user can scale the plan. Use the user's historical weekday rankings only as a tie-breaker: YouTube's differences are modest, while TikTok/Instagram patterns are confounded by existing posting days. Balance pillars and formats without imposing arbitrary percentages or a post-every-three-days rule.
4. Include at least one YouTube Community post every Friday at 23:00 GMT+8, with optional additional posts. Rotate polls, still-image concepts, character/lore choices, theory prompts, quizzes, and genuine production updates; label any new visual or behind-the-scenes asset as a production need rather than assuming it exists. Do not rely on announcement-only posts. Treat YouTube Shorts slots at 18:00, 23:00, or 00:00 GMT+8 as experiments, not optimized times; show 00:00 on its actual date.
5. Adapt content and interactions to each active platform instead of recycling identical feed posts. Include available native activities (such as Stories, polls, and audience replies) when supported by the account, and distinguish them from published feed assets. Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook are in scope, but Facebook is a completely new page, not an established peer of the other accounts. Plan a distinct Facebook launch/onboarding track first: explain Somex and Quantum 13 to first-time visitors, orient them to the released episodes and where to start, then trial selected standalone highlights with context. Do not mirror the established channels' clip cadence, assume an audience or posting history, or claim a best Facebook day/time. Suggest only the Facebook activity that serves a clear new-page purpose; additional slots are optional, not parity targets. Mark any introductory graphics/video or page details as needed unless confirmed available. Reddit means researched participation in established communities for discoverability/SEO, not posting to a Somex-owned subreddit; follow each community's rules and never promise indexing or rankings. Do not schedule Discord, X/Twitter, or LinkedIn unless the user explicitly changes the channel strategy.
6. Keep Sunday entirely off: no proposed posts, videos, Stories, polls, Community items, Reddit activity, or flexible slots on Sundays (GMT+8). Validate the proposed weekly/monthly calendar against four hard rules before showing it: Sunday has no scheduled or proposed activity; preserve episode order across all planned video highlights (within an episode, cuts may shuffle, but Episode 06 highlights cannot precede any scheduled Episode 05 highlight); no platform publishes videos on consecutive dates (Saturday-to-Monday is permitted when Sunday is skipped); and Instagram and TikTok never publish **videos** on the same date. Stories and polls are exempt from both video-spacing and Instagram/TikTok same-date video checks; a TikTok video and Instagram Story on the same date are allowed. YouTube Shorts and Community posts may be on adjacent days. Check cross-week boundaries as described in `references/analytics-and-scheduling.md`. Protect the mandatory Friday 23:00 GMT+8 YouTube Community slot. Leave flexible slots where useful without treating them as a required percentage, and never assign one to a conflicting date. Recommend content even if it needs new production; the user will decide what to make and may adjust the workload. Do not prescribe batching sessions or time blocks unless asked.
7. Mark every item as idea, draft, approved, scheduled, or published only when that state is actually known. Never claim publishing, queue placement, analytics, or approval without verification. Draft first; obtain explicit approval before scheduling or publishing through a verified connected tool; read back the queue before claiming success.
8. Review actual platform metrics and audience replies weekly when supplied; recalibrate the mix monthly as appropriate. Distinguish studio replies from audience sentiment. Adjust future slots based on evidence, not unverified algorithm prescriptions.

## Output

Default to a compact in-chat calendar, not a file, unless explicitly asked for a document. Group entries by GMT+8 date. Begin each entry with the user's requested classification **Platform - Content, type** (e.g., `YouTube - Community Post, poll`; `YouTube - Video, YouTube Short`; `Instagram - Story, video`; `Instagram - Story, picture`). Then add a concise concept/hook, source, goal, and draft status; include production/approval needs only where relevant. Name the placement clearly (e.g., Instagram Feed Post/Reel versus Story; Facebook Feed Post versus Video) and keep the second field as the content container and the third as its actual type, not an invented asset state. Keep placeholders visible and list the few decisions needed to operationalize the draft. Do not silently convert a proposed plan into an approved schedule.
