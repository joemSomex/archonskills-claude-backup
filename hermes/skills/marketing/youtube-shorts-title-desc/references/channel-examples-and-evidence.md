## Decision boundary

The channel evidence supports drawing inspiration from clip-specific hooks and a navigation path to longer content. It does **not** establish that title length, the repeated series phrase, synopsis length, link placement, or hashtags caused the view differences. Different clip lengths, topics, ages, and audiences confound such an inference. Future per-video analytics can refine the skill; until then describe any proposed optimization as a testable hypothesis.
