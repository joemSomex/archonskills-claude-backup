# YouTube Long-Form Algorithm Guide

Source basis: *Somex Report — YouTube Algorithm: Long-Form*, prepared 6 July 2026. The report treats YouTube Help, How YouTube Works, and the official YouTube Blog as authoritative where sources conflict, with secondary sources used only for corroboration. Platform behavior changes; verify current product features and thresholds when materially important.

## Core model

YouTube operates multiple recommendation systems rather than one algorithm. Home, Suggested, Search, Shorts, Subscriptions, Notifications, and external traffic have different mechanics. Recommendations are personalized: the system selects videos for each viewer based on likely satisfaction at that moment.

The useful creator question is not “Does the algorithm like this video?” but “Which viewers will enjoy this, and what evidence shows that they did?”

Primary video-level signals:

- Impressions and click-through rate (CTR)
- Watch time, average view duration, and audience retention
- Satisfaction signals such as likes, dislikes, surveys, shares, comments, continued viewing, “Not interested,” and “Don’t recommend channel”

YouTube reports that half of channels and videos have an impressions CTR between 2% and 10%, but this is contextual rather than a target. CTR normally falls when a successful video expands from loyal viewers to broader audiences. Compare CTR by traffic surface and alongside impression volume.

## Surfaces

- **Home:** personalized from watch history and performance among similar viewers.
- **Suggested / Up Next:** strongly influenced by the currently watched video, topical relationships, and common next-view behavior.
- **Search:** relevance of title, description, tags, and spoken content, plus engagement from the query and channel trust.
- **Shorts:** separate swipe-feed system; viewed-versus-swiped-away and completion are central, while thumbnail CTR does not apply in-feed.
- **Subscriptions:** recent uploads from subscribed channels.
- **Notifications:** primarily reach subscribers who actively watch the channel.
- **External:** comes from websites, chats, or other platforms and is not itself ranked by YouTube.

For episodic stories, Suggested and Home are usually the main growth surfaces. Search is more valuable for evergreen, query-driven subjects. Shorts are best used for discovery and introduction.

## Upload lifecycle

A new upload is first shown to a modest likely audience. YouTube reads clicks, viewing behavior, and satisfaction, then widens or narrows exposure. Testing can continue for hours, days, months, or longer. Videos have no fixed expiry and can resurface when viewer interest returns.

Treat each upload as fresh performance data. One failed experiment does not doom the channel. Avoid attributing normal seasonal or topic-driven variation to punishment without evidence.

## Long-form rules

Video length is not a ranking factor. A long video can generate more total watch minutes, but only if retention holds. Padding causes exits and weakens future recommendation evidence.

The 8-minute threshold concerns eligibility for mid-roll advertisements, not organic recommendation. Place ads at natural pauses and review automatic placements when monetization is active.

Retention interpretation:

- Early declines are normal; reduce them with a strong promise or immediate story movement.
- Flat sections indicate sustained attention.
- Dips indicate possible boredom, confusion, repetition, or interruption.
- Spikes indicate replaying or navigation to a moment and can inform Shorts, hooks, thumbnails, and future creative decisions.

Only private YouTube Studio retention data can establish actual retention peaks or most-replayed moments.

## Language strategy

Viewer language affects recommendation and search matching. Use corrected subtitles, translated titles, and translated descriptions for priority audiences. Multi-language audio keeps language versions on one upload and can simplify management and analytics compared with separate channels.

The report cites YouTube’s claim that creators using multi-language audio saw, on average, more than 25% of watch time from non-original languages. It also reports that deep dubbing coverage performs better than occasional dubbing; prioritize one or two languages and cover most relevant catalog content.

For English and Mandarin, use aligned audio, subtitles, titles, and descriptions. Mandarin reach on YouTube mainly comes from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, and the global diaspora because YouTube is inaccessible in mainland China.

## Upload checklist

Before upload:

1. Design title and thumbnail as one honest promise.
2. Keep thumbnails simple, high-contrast, and readable on phones.
3. Put the hook or story movement in the first 15–30 seconds.
4. Keep one clear idea or story promise per video.

During upload:

5. Put the main subject and emotional promise early in the title; roughly 60 characters is a useful truncation guideline, not an absolute rule.
6. Use the first description lines to explain the video clearly; place chapters, links, and credits afterward.
7. Add chapters to useful long-form videos.
8. Use only accurate tags; tags are a minor signal and help mainly with variants and spellings.
9. Add or correct captions, translated metadata, and multi-language audio where available.
10. Point cards and end screens to the next logical video or series playlist.

After publishing:

11. Pin a useful comment and reply to early viewers.
12. Test thumbnails with YouTube Studio when available.
13. Avoid judging from the first small batch of impressions; allow enough time and data.
14. Review Reach and Engagement regularly: impressions plus CTR diagnose packaging, while retention diagnoses the video.
15. Maintain a sustainable rhythm; consistency trains audience habits but is not itself a ranking boost.
16. Experiment deliberately and retain what the data supports.

## Important distinctions

- Falling CTR plus rapidly growing impressions can indicate successful audience expansion.
- Upload time has little known effect on long-term performance; scheduling matters more for Premieres and live streams.
- Subscriber count is not a direct ranking input, though active subscribers form a useful first-test audience.
- Paid promotion does not directly boost or damage organic ranking.
- Advertiser suitability and recommendation suitability are related policy concerns but not the same system.
- Entertainment is primarily judged by relevance, freshness, popularity, viewing behavior, and satisfaction; authoritative-source weighting is more pronounced in sensitive information categories.
