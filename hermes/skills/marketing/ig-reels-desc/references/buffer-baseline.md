# Buffer baseline for Instagram Reel captions

Source: https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/ . The article was fetched and read during this conversation. It is third-party guidance, not verified Somex performance or blanket official policy. The user requested it as the starting point for this skill and explicitly withheld finalization.

## Useful starting principles

- Instagram ranks Feed, Stories, Reels, and Explore differently. For this skill focus on Reel discovery, not a universal multi-format content plan.
- Buffer highlights average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach, reporting Mosseri's emphasis on likes for followers and sends for non-followers. No exact ranking weights are disclosed. Treat these as strategic signals, not a numerical scoring formula.
- A Reel and its caption should communicate their subject clearly to people unfamiliar with the account. Natural topic terms and character context can help comprehension; do not imply keywords guarantee recommendation or search ranking.
- Share-worthiness may come from a relatable, striking, funny, or thought-provoking moment. Write around the actual moment rather than adding generic prompts to share.
- Original owned work and clean cross-platform masters are useful. A third-party app watermark is not the same as Somex's own branding. Caption writing does not itself verify originality, video quality, audio rights, or asset readiness.
- Review actual post performance and normalize relevant response metrics by reach. Different surface, topic, audience, and post age can confound results. Do not equate aggregate shares with private sends unless the metric explicitly supports it.

## Do not convert into hard rules

- Buffer's broad format percentages and best-time studies do not establish Somex's best format or time. Its 12% engagement comparison and 109% engagement-per-person-reached figure use different denominators.
- The reported association between comment replies and engagement is not causal proof. No reply quota, required CTA, posting frequency, or Sunday activity is adopted.
- The article's feature-availability, five-hashtag cap, recommendation-eligibility and other current-policy claims need current official Instagram/Meta checks before enforcement. No fixed hashtag count is approved here.
- Trending audio, trial Reels and follow-up timing are potential experiments outside caption-only scope, not requirements or guaranteed reach boosts. Do not override existing video spacing to chase momentum.
- Violent or sensitive footage may have recommendation limitations; check official guidance and account eligibility when relevant rather than guaranteeing reach or treating a caption as a fix.
