# Waterfall Content Strategy

Turn one substantial hero asset into a coordinated stream of smaller content that extends its reach, lifespan, and audience engagement.

Trigger on “waterfall this video,” “build a waterfall content plan,” “repurpose this episode,” “create content from this hero asset,” or `/waterfall-content-strategy`.

## Core principle

Start with one source asset, extract its strongest moments and ideas, adapt them into native formats, and distribute them over time. Do not merely repost the same promotion. Make every derivative asset useful or entertaining on its own while naturally leading interested viewers back to the hero content or wider story world.

For the foundational definition and three-stage model, consult [Folly Creative’s waterfall-content overview](https://www.follycreative.co.uk/blog/what-is-a-waterfall-content-strategy-and-why-should-i-be-using-it) when deeper background is needed.

## Workflow

### 1. Establish the hero asset

Identify:

- The main video, episode, podcast, article, launch, or campaign.
- The primary audience objective: discovery, watch time, conversation, conversion, retention, or reactivation.
- The strongest scenes, ideas, quotes, characters, conflicts, reveals, questions, visuals, sounds, and emotional beats.
- Any spoilers, rights restrictions, canon boundaries, platform limitations, or timing constraints.

If the source cannot be watched or read directly, state the limitation and use only supplied transcripts, comments, analytics, descriptions, or approved source material. Never invent moments or timestamps.

### 2. Build the content bank

Extract reusable material before proposing formats:

- Hooks and opening lines
- Short clips and visual moments
- Character-focused moments
- Quotes and dialogue
- Lore, facts, and explanations
- Questions, theories, and unresolved mysteries
- Music, sound, and atmosphere
- Production or behind-the-scenes material
- Audience reactions and user-generated content opportunities

Separate confirmed source material from new creative suggestions. Preserve canon exactly when working with a fictional universe.

### 3. Design the waterfall

Develop assets in three layers:

1. **Discovery assets** — Short, immediate pieces designed to reach new viewers: Shorts, clips, striking images, hooks, or standalone moments.
2. **Engagement assets** — Native conversation formats: polls, lore questions, character choices, theories, quizzes, comparisons, and audience prompts.
3. **Depth and conversion assets** — Recaps, explainers, behind-the-scenes material, music features, compilations, newsletters, or articles that deepen interest and direct qualified viewers toward the hero asset.

Adapt each asset to its platform rather than copying identical text everywhere. Match the hook, length, framing, caption, and call to action to how people use that surface.

### 4. Match assets to the active platforms

- **YouTube:** Use full episodes, trailers, compilations, recaps, explainers, selected Shorts, and Community posts. Treat it as the main home for long-form, search-driven, and cinematic content.
- **Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook Reels:** Use immediately understandable short-form moments. Add burned-in captions and prominent hook text because viewers frequently encounter these clips without sound.
- **Reddit:** Use context-rich clips, lore discussions, theories, production insights, and genuine participation adapted to each relevant subreddit. Avoid promotional dumping and follow subreddit rules.

Do not recommend X/Twitter, LinkedIn, or Discord for Quantum XIII unless the approved channel strategy changes.

### 5. Treat Community posts as core distribution

Use YouTube Community posts consistently as part of the waterfall, not as an optional extra. Prioritize posts that create native engagement:

- Polls
- Character choices
- Lore questions
- Viewer theories
- Images and visual reveals
- Quizzes
- Behind-the-scenes details

Avoid announcement-only posts whose entire message is “watch the latest video.” Make the post interesting without requiring a click, then connect it naturally to the relevant video or series.

### 6. Sequence the release

Spread the waterfall before, during, and after the hero release:

- **Before:** curiosity, character setup, questions, visuals, and anticipation.
- **Launch:** strongest hook, trailer moment, contextual post, and clear viewing path.
- **After:** reactions, theories, explanations, music, character focus, recaps, and evergreen rediscovery assets.

Use GMT+8 for Somex Studios schedules and daily boundaries unless instructed otherwise. Keep strong evergreen assets in rotation instead of abandoning them after one week.

### 7. Measure and improve

Track results by asset and format:

- Reach and impressions
- Viewer retention or completion
- Comments and meaningful replies
- Shares and saves
- Click-through or movement toward the hero asset
- Returning-viewer behavior
- Recurring questions, characters, or themes

For YouTube long-form, separate packaging from content: impressions plus CTR indicate whether the title and thumbnail attract qualified viewers, while retention indicates whether the video fulfills the promise. Interpret CTR by traffic surface and alongside impression growth.

Use private retention data to select waterfall assets: spikes are candidates for Shorts, trailers, thumbnails, and future hooks; dips identify confusing or slow moments to avoid repeating. Never claim a most-replayed moment from comments or raw playback alone.

For episodic content, connect every asset to the next logical episode or ordered series playlist. Treat Shorts as discovery, not proof that the same audience will watch long-form.

Use performance to adjust the next waterfall. Preserve successful hooks and formats in the content bank; retire weak executions rather than abandoning the underlying idea immediately.

## Quantum XIII constraints

- Treat each episode, trailer, music video, or major lore release as a potential hero asset.
- Keep confirmed canon separate from speculative prompts and audience theories.
- Favor character, lore, mystery, music, and cinematic-world assets over generic promotional reminders.
- Do not recommend Discord distribution or a Quantum XIII Discord community. Quantum XIII currently has no plans to operate one; revisit only after an explicit strategy change.
- Do not recommend X/Twitter or LinkedIn distribution. There are no current plans for a Quantum XIII X account, and LinkedIn is excluded from the content strategy.
- Treat YouTube Community posts as a core growth channel.
- Exclude Somex Studios’ own replies when using comment sentiment to choose waterfall topics, unless the task specifically analyzes studio activity.

## Output

Default to a concise response in chat unless a document is explicitly requested. Include only what the task needs, typically:

- Hero asset and objective
- Core audience hooks
- Recommended derivative assets
- Distribution sequence
- Community-post concepts
- Metrics to watch

Distinguish source-backed observations from creative recommendations. Flag missing source access, incomplete comment coverage, or absent analytics.
