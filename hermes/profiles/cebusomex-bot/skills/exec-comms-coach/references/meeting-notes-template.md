# Meeting notes template and how to build the record

Use in Prep (give the user a blank sheet), Debrief (turn rough notes into this), and Coach (when the user asks how to prove what was said).

## Why notes fail (patterns seen in the user's real notes)
- Fragments with no owner or deadline ("subtitles", "timecodes", "sen: tiktok and ig posting").
- Numbers with no meaning attached ("IG max 3 mins": a limit or a target? "16:9, 21:9": required or suggested?).
- Blank items ("streak explanation -", "achievements badge -").
- Heard, decided and suggested are mixed together, so a remark reads like a decision.
- No scope tag. China and global plans differ, and mixing them caused a real mistake.
- No date or attendees on some meetings. Names that differ between notes ("director" vs a first name) go unchecked.
- Notes end when the meeting ends. No written confirmation goes out afterwards.

## Sheet to use
Meeting: [who] | Date: [date] | Scope: China / Global / Both

| # | What was said (exact words for decisions) | Type | Scope | Owner | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Type**: Decision, Task, Suggestion, Info, or Question.
- **Status**: Confirmed (they said it clearly), Assumed (I inferred it), Open (unclear or missing).
- Put exact words in quotes for decisions and instructions. Paraphrase everything else.
- If a note has no owner or due date, mark Open. Do not fill it in.
- Leave a line at the bottom for "Ask before I leave": the two or three things to verify while everyone is still there.

## After the meeting
The organized notes go to the group chat. They are the record, so post them the same day, in full plain sentences, with the date and attendees at the top. Use the Confirmed / Assumed / Open table only for private planning or when the user asks. If something in the notes is still unknown after the user answers, write it as a follow-up item, not as a decision.

## Keeping proof
Proof is built going forward, not found in the past.
- The organized notes in the group chat are the record. A reply or a lack of correction, with the Lark timestamp, shows what was agreed.
- Keep one central log of decisions with date, who said it, scope, and the source (meeting or message).
- When guidance seems to conflict, do not claim "you said X". Ask: "on [date] we agreed [A]. on [date] i understood [B]. which one applies to [case]?" Then log the answer.
- Old meetings with no record cannot be proven. Ask the question instead of guessing.

## Worked example (from the user's own notes, gaps left as Open)
Meeting: Jason + Silvia | Date: not noted | Scope: not noted

| # | What was said | Type | Scope | Owner | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subtitles | ? | ? | ? | ? | Open: on which platforms, required or suggested? |
| 2 | 16:9 and 21:9 for YouTube | Info | Global? | ? | ? | Open: format to make, or just what YouTube accepts? |
| 3 | Timecodes | ? | ? | ? | ? | Open: for what? |
| 4 | IG max 3 min, TikTok 1 min | Info | Global | ? | ? | Open: limit or target length? |
| 5 | Sen: TikTok and IG posting | Task | Global | Sen | ? | Open: due date? |
| 6 | Catch-up posts for the global side | Task | Global | ? | ? | Open: who, and by when? |

Ask before I leave: (1) who owns items 1 to 3? (2) is 1 to 4 a rule or a suggestion? (3) due date for 5 and 6?
